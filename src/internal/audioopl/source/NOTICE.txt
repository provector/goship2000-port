The standalone helper links compiler/runtime support from official Zig0.13.0.
Accompanying runtime sources and their original license notices are retained in
 audio-helper-runtime-sources.tar.gz. These include LLVM libc++/libc++abi/libunwind
(Apache2 with LLVM exceptions and retained legacy notices), musl (MIT), MinGW
(its COPYING and per-file notices), and Zig runtime sources (MIT/per-file notices).
The archive preserves individual notices rather than replacing them with this
short summary. Windows UCRT/KERNEL32 are OS-provided APIs; no Microsoft DLL is
redistributed here. Target Windows10+; execution on Windows remains unverified.
